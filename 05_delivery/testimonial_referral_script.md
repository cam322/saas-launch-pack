# Testimonial + Referral Scripts

## After free audit
Glad this was useful. If the audit gave you a clear next step, could I quote you on that? One sentence is perfect.

## After paid audit
Was this useful enough to recommend to another founder? If yes, could you send one sentence about what changed or what became clearer?

## Testimonial prompt
“Before the audit, we struggled with _____. After the audit, we knew _____. I’d recommend it because _____.”

## Referral ask
Do you know 1–2 other SaaS founders who might want a 60-second conversion audit? A simple intro is enough:

“Hey — this is {{your_name}}. They did a quick conversion audit for us and found a useful fix. Might be worth a look.”

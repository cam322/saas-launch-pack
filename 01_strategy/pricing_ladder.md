# Pricing Ladder

The kit sells a process. The service sells implementation.

## Tier 0 — Free audit teaser
Price: free  
Goal: get replies and prove relevance.

Deliver:
- 1 specific issue
- 1 screenshot or timestamp
- 1 suggested fix
- CTA: “Want the full 3-point audit?”

## Tier 1 — Quick-Win Audit
Price: $299  
Goal: first revenue.

Deliver:
- 3-point conversion audit
- prioritized fix list
- 1 quick fix spec or done-for-you change
- 15-minute walkthrough

## Tier 2 — Implementation Sprint
Price: $799  
Goal: higher ticket from audit buyers.

Deliver:
- implement top 1–3 fixes
- copy polish
- signup/demo CTA cleanup
- analytics/event checklist
- before/after summary

## Tier 3 — Growth Ops Retainer
Price: $1,500/mo  
Goal: recurring revenue.

Deliver:
- monthly conversion review
- 2 tests/month
- outreach/landing iteration
- weekly KPI report

## Price raise rule
After 3 paid pilots:
- $299 → $499
- $799 → $1,200
- $1,500/mo → $2,000/mo

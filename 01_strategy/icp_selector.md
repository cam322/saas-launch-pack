# ICP Selector

Score each possible niche from 1–5. Pick the highest score.

## Criteria
1. Easy to find online
2. Clear buyer/contact person
3. Pain visible from website/product flow
4. Has money or revenue pressure
5. Can buy without procurement
6. You can deliver a quick win in 3 days
7. There are 50+ prospects available

## Scoring table
| Niche | Findability | Buyer clarity | Visible pain | Money | Fast buy | Fast delivery | Prospect volume | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Project management SaaS for agencies | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 31 |
| CRM tools for local service businesses | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 28 |
| AI writing tools for creators | 5 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 26 |

## Decision rule
- 28+ = greenlight
- 22–27 = test only if you already know the market
- under 22 = skip

## Best first ICP
Tiny B2B SaaS teams with a live product, weak landing/onboarding flow, and founder-led sales.

# Offer Canvas

Use this before writing any outreach. If this page is vague, the whole launch fails.

## 1. Niche
Pick one narrow buyer group.

Bad: SaaS companies  
Good: project management tools for 2–15 person digital agencies

**Chosen niche:**  
`[write niche]`

## 2. Painful outcome
What measurable business pain do they feel?

Examples:
- Trial users do not convert to paid.
- Demo requests do not show up.
- Onboarding feels confusing.
- Landing page traffic does not turn into calls.

**Pain:**  
`[write pain]`

## 3. Micro-offer
The first paid offer must be small, specific, and fast.

Template:
> I will help `[niche]` achieve `[measurable outcome]` in `[timeframe]` by delivering `[quick win]`.

Example:
> I help project management SaaS teams find and fix one trial-to-paid conversion leak in 3 business days.

**Offer:**  
`[write offer]`

## 4. Deliverable
What exactly does the customer receive?

Default:
- 60-second Loom-style audit
- 3 friction points
- 1 priority fix
- Implementation notes
- Optional done-for-you fix

**Deliverable:**  
`[write deliverable]`

## 5. Price ladder
- Free sample: 60-second audit teaser
- Paid pilot: $299 audit + fix plan
- Implementation: $799 quick implementation sprint
- Retainer: $1,500/mo conversion ops

## 6. Risk reversal
Choose one:
- If I do not find one actionable fix, you pay $0.
- If the audit is generic, you pay $0.
- If you do not get a prioritized implementation plan, you pay $0.

## 7. Proof
Even without testimonials, use process proof:
- Before/after screenshots
- Checklist screenshots
- Specific teardown examples
- Public UX principles
- Mini case study from first free audits

## Final one-liner
> We help `[niche]` fix `[pain]` with a `[timeframe]` quick-win audit, no ad spend or long consulting engagement.

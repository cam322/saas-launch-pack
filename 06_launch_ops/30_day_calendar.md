# 30-Day Launch Calendar

## Week 1 — Build signal
Day 1: pick niche, fill offer canvas, build landing page.  
Day 2: build 50-prospect list.  
Day 3: send first 10 emails + 10 LinkedIn notes.  
Day 4: send next 10, reply to anyone interested.  
Day 5: send next 10, complete first free audits.  
Day 6: send next 10, ask interested leads for paid audit.  
Day 7: send next 10, review reply data.

## Week 2 — Convert
Day 8: follow-up touch 2 to first 20.  
Day 9: deliver free audit teasers.  
Day 10: pitch $299 audits.  
Day 11: follow-up touch 2 to next 20.  
Day 12: book calls.  
Day 13: close paid pilots.  
Day 14: deliver first paid audit.

## Week 3 — Proof
Day 15–17: deliver paid audits.  
Day 18: collect testimonials.  
Day 19: add proof to landing page.  
Day 20: build second prospect list.  
Day 21: raise price if 3 paid wins.

## Week 4 — Scale what worked
Day 22–25: send 15/day using winning message.  
Day 26: package case study.  
Day 27: upsell implementation sprint.  
Day 28: ask for referrals.  
Day 29: tighten landing page.  
Day 30: decide: double down, pivot niche, or productize further.

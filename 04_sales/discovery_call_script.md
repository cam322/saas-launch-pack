# 15-Minute Discovery Call Script

## Goal
Qualify fast, sell a low-risk paid pilot, avoid unpaid consulting.

## Minute 0–2 — Frame
“Thanks for taking the time. I’ll keep this tight. I want to understand your conversion path, show what I noticed, and if useful, suggest the smallest paid test.”

## Minute 2–6 — Diagnose
Ask:
1. What is the main conversion goal right now — trial, demo, paid signup?
2. Where do prospects usually drop off?
3. How many visitors/trials/demos do you get monthly?
4. Who is the best-fit buyer?
5. Have you tested the current headline/CTA/onboarding?

## Minute 6–10 — Show insight
Share one specific issue from your scan:
- what you noticed
- why it matters
- what you would test first

## Minute 10–13 — Offer
“The smallest useful next step is a $299 quick-win audit. I’ll deliver 3 friction points, pick the highest-leverage fix, and give you implementation notes within 3 business days. If I do not find one actionable fix, you pay $0.”

## Minute 13–15 — Close
Ask:
“Want me to run that this week?”

If yes:
- confirm email
- confirm URL/path to review
- send payment/proposal
- schedule walkthrough

If maybe:
- send written proposal
- follow up in 24 hours

# Cold Email Cadence

Rules:
- 70–110 words max.
- One specific observation.
- One low-friction ask.
- No fake case studies.
- No calendar link until they show interest.

## Email 1 — Specific audit offer
Subject: quick fix for {{company}}

Hi {{first_name}},

I looked at {{company}} and noticed {{one_line_issue}}.

One quick test: {{suggested_fix}}.

I’m putting together short 60-second conversion audits for {{niche}} founders. Want me to send you the quick audit for {{company}}? No call needed.

— {{your_name}}

## Email 2 — Bump with value
Subject: {{company}} audit note

{{first_name}}, quick bump.

The reason I flagged this: {{business_reason}}. Small CTA/onboarding friction can quietly kill trial-to-paid conversion.

I can send the 60-second version with the top 3 fixes. Want it?

— {{your_name}}

## Email 3 — Paid pilot ask
Subject: 3-day quick win

If improving {{company}}’s signup/demo flow is worth testing, I can do the full audit + priority fix plan for $299.

You get:
- 3 conversion leaks
- 1 priority fix
- implementation notes
- 15-minute walkthrough

If I do not find one actionable fix, you pay $0.

Worth testing?

## Email 4 — Proof/process
Subject: my process

{{first_name}}, here’s exactly how the audit works:

1. I scan homepage + signup/demo path.
2. I identify the highest-friction step.
3. I give you one change to test first.

No deck. No long engagement. Just a practical fix.

Want me to send the free teaser?

## Email 5 — Breakup
Subject: close the loop?

Should I close the loop on this?

If conversion cleanup is not a priority right now, no worries. If it is, I can send the 60-second audit for {{company}} this week.

— {{your_name}}

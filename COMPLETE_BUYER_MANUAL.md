A sellable, execution-first launch kit for early-stage SaaS founders who need their first paid customers without ads, agencies, or complicated funnels.

# ICP Selector

Score each possible niche from 1–5. Pick the highest score.

## Criteria
1. Easy to find online
2. Clear buyer/contact person
3. Pain visible from website/product flow
4. Has money or revenue pressure
5. Can buy without procurement
6. You can deliver a quick win in 3 days
7. There are 50+ prospects available

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

- 28+ = greenlight

Tiny B2B SaaS teams with a live product, weak landing/onboarding flow, and founder-led sales.

# Prospecting SOP: First 50 Targets

## Goal
Build 50 relevant prospects in one focused session. Do not over-research.

## Target filters
Use companies that have:
- Live SaaS website
- Clear signup/demo CTA
- Founder, growth, product, or marketing contact visible
- Small team or founder-led sales motion
- Obvious conversion friction you can mention

## Free sources
- GitHub orgs and product links
- Product Hunt launches
- Indie Hackers posts
- LinkedIn company search
- SaaS directories
- X/Twitter bios
- Google: `site:producthunt.com "project management" "SaaS"`

## Data columns
- Company
- Website
- Prospect name
- Title
- Email
- LinkedIn
- Public signal
- One-line issue
- Suggested fix
- Stage
- Last contacted
- Reply
- Revenue
- Notes

## 60-second scan process
1. Open homepage.
2. Find primary CTA.
3. Check if value prop is clear in 5 seconds.
4. Click signup/demo if public.
5. Note one friction point.
6. Write one specific fix.

## Quality bar
Every prospect needs one non-generic line:
> “I noticed your homepage CTA says ‘Get Started’ but does not say what happens next. I’d test ‘Start a 14-day client workflow trial’ for agency buyers.”

## Stop condition
When you have 50 rows, stop researching and start sending.

# 60-Second SaaS Site Scan Checklist

Use this to create the personalization line.

## Above the fold
- Is the buyer named?
- Is the outcome specific?
- Is the CTA visible without scrolling?
- Does the CTA explain next step?
- Is there social proof near CTA?

## Offer clarity
- Can I explain what they do in 5 seconds?
- Is pricing easy to find?
- Is there a demo/trial choice conflict?
- Are features listed before outcomes?

## Conversion friction
- Too many CTAs
- Generic headline
- No proof
- No clear ICP
- Signup asks too much too soon
- Demo form has too many fields
- No “what happens next” text

## One-line fix formulas
- “I’d change `[generic CTA]` to `[specific CTA]` so buyers know the next step.”
- “I’d move `[proof]` next to the CTA to reduce risk before signup.”
- “I’d rewrite the headline from `[feature]` to `[outcome]` for `[ICP]`.”
- “I’d reduce the demo form from `[N]` fields to `[N]` fields to lower friction.”

## Output
Company:  
Issue:  
Fix:  
CTA for outreach: `Want me to send the 60-second audit?`

# Cold Email Cadence

Rules:
- 70–110 words max.
- One specific observation.
- One low-friction ask.
- No fake case studies.
- No calendar link until they show interest.

## Email 1 — Specific audit offer
Subject: quick fix for {{company}}

I looked at {{company}} and noticed {{one_line_issue}}.

One quick test: {{suggested_fix}}.

I’m putting together short 60-second conversion audits for {{niche}} founders. Want me to send you the quick audit for {{company}}? No call needed.

— {{your_name}}

Subject: {{company}} audit note

{{first_name}}, quick bump.

The reason I flagged this: {{business_reason}}. Small CTA/onboarding friction can quietly kill trial-to-paid conversion.

I can send the 60-second version with the top 3 fixes. Want it?

— {{your_name}}

# Personalization Bank

## Public signals
- “Saw your Product Hunt launch.”
- “Noticed your GitHub repo links to the product site.”
- “Saw your founder post about onboarding.”
- “Noticed you target agencies on the homepage.”
- “Saw pricing is self-serve but demo CTA is still primary.”

## One-line issue examples
- “Your CTA says ‘Get Started’ but does not explain what starts.”
- “The hero says what the product is, but not who it is for.”
- “The demo form asks for 6 fields before explaining what happens next.”
- “The pricing page has three plans but no recommended option.”
- “The signup flow starts before the buyer sees proof.”

## Suggested fixes
- “Test a CTA that names the next step: ‘Start a 14-day agency workflow trial.’”
- “Add one proof line directly under the CTA.”
- “Move the ICP into the headline.”
- “Cut the demo form to name + email + company.”
- “Add ‘What happens after you book’ under the demo button.”

## Business reasons
- “Unclear CTAs create hesitation.”
- “Buyers need to self-identify quickly.”
- “Long forms add friction before trust is established.”
- “Proof near the CTA reduces perceived risk.”

# Objection Responses

## “No budget”
Totally fair. That is why I start with a free 60-second teaser. If the fix is obvious and useful, the paid audit is $299. If not, you move on with a free idea.

## “Send info”
Sure. The short version: I find 3 conversion leaks in your signup/demo path and give you the first fix to test. Delivered in 3 business days. Want me to start with the free 60-second teaser for {{company}}?

## “We already have a designer/marketer”
Perfect — this is not meant to replace them. It gives them a prioritized outside view and one concrete fix to test.

## “Not a priority”
Understood. When conversion becomes a priority, the easiest first step is a 60-second audit. Want me to send one now so you have it on file?

## “What results can you guarantee?”
I cannot guarantee a conversion lift without traffic and implementation. I can guarantee a specific, actionable audit. If I do not find one useful fix, you pay $0.

## “How long does it take?”
Free teaser: usually same day. Paid quick-win audit: 3 business days.

# 15-Minute Discovery Call Script

## Goal
Qualify fast, sell a low-risk paid pilot, avoid unpaid consulting.

## Minute 0–2 — Frame
“Thanks for taking the time. I’ll keep this tight. I want to understand your conversion path, show what I noticed, and if useful, suggest the smallest paid test.”

## Minute 2–6 — Diagnose
Ask:
1. What is the main conversion goal right now — trial, demo, paid signup?
2. Where do prospects usually drop off?
3. How many visitors/trials/demos do you get monthly?
4. Who is the best-fit buyer?
5. Have you tested the current headline/CTA/onboarding?

## Minute 6–10 — Show insight
Share one specific issue from your scan:
- what you noticed
- why it matters
- what you would test first

## Minute 10–13 — Offer
“The smallest useful next step is a $299 quick-win audit. I’ll deliver 3 friction points, pick the highest-leverage fix, and give you implementation notes within 3 business days. If I do not find one actionable fix, you pay $0.”

## Minute 13–15 — Close
Ask:
“Want me to run that this week?”

If yes:
- confirm email
- confirm URL/path to review
- send payment/proposal
- schedule walkthrough

If maybe:
- send written proposal
- follow up in 24 hours

# 3-Day Delivery SOP

## Day 1 — Audit
- Confirm goal and URL.
- Screenshot homepage, CTA, signup/demo path.
- Identify 5–10 issues.
- Pick top 3.
- Pick one priority fix.

## Day 2 — Package
- Write audit using `04_sales/audit_delivery_template.md`.
- Add screenshots or links.
- Turn vague advice into exact copy/design recommendations.
- Prepare walkthrough notes.

## Day 3 — Deliver and upsell
- Send audit.
- Walk through top fix.
- Ask if they want implementation.
- Offer $799 sprint.
- Ask for testimonial if they got value.

## Quality bar
Never deliver generic advice like “improve CTA.” Deliver exact copy:
Bad: “Make CTA clearer.”  
Good: “Change ‘Get Started’ to ‘Start a 14-day agency workflow trial’.”

# KPI Dashboard

Track weekly. No vibes.

## Core metrics
- Prospects added
- Emails sent
- LinkedIn DMs sent
- Replies
- Positive replies
- Calls booked
- Paid audits sold
- Implementation sprints sold
- Revenue
- Hours spent

## Decision rules
After 50 prospects:
- <2 replies: niche or personalization is wrong.
- 2–5 replies but no calls: offer/CTA is weak.
- Calls but no sales: price, trust, or discovery script is weak.
- Sales but poor delivery: tighten SOP.

## Targets for first 30 days
- 50–150 prospects
- 5–15 replies
- 2–6 calls
- 1–3 paid audits
- $299–$897 first revenue

## Weekly review questions
1. Which subject line got replies?
2. Which ICP replied fastest?
3. Which objection repeated?
4. Which audit issue appeared most often?
5. What one change improves next week?

# Founder Daily Checklist

45 minutes/day.

## 10 minutes — Pipeline
- Add 5–10 prospects.
- Verify each has one specific hook.

## 20 minutes — Sending
- Send today’s emails.
- Send LinkedIn notes/DMs.
- Log stage and date.

## 10 minutes — Replies
- Answer positive replies first.
- Move interested leads to call or paid audit.
- Handle objections with the response bank.

## 5 minutes — Scoreboard
Update:
- sent
- replies
- calls
- revenue
- notes

## Rule
Do not spend the daily block redesigning the landing page. Revenue comes from conversations.
